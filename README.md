Pixel Kingdom‌ is a goofy retro kingdom management sim stuffed into a single HTML file. No install, no setup — just copy the web adress https://horzionexpress.github.io/pixel-kindon-game/  and immediately get to bossing around little pixel people, starting wars, and hoarding gold like a tiny medieval dragon. It's basically Civilization, Age of Empires, and your childhood pixel art obsession crammed into one browser tab. 🏰✨

How to rule your kingdom

Click empty tiles to plop down farms, mines, houses and barracks to get your economy chugging

Use the sidebar to manage government, research tech, negotiate with neighbors, or declare war on whoever looked at you funny

Build your army from peasant militias to knights and siege engines, then march them off to conquer land

Drag to pan the map, scroll to zoom, hit speed controls to fast forward through boring parts

Keep your people fed and happy… or don't. Peasant revolts are actually kind of fun to crush, no judgment.

What's packed in this mess

20+ building types for production, housing, defense and military

Full diplomacy system: form alliances, sign trade deals, make vassals, or declare total war

8 unit types from militia to knights, catapults, and royal guards

Tech tree with economy, military and quality of life upgrades

Dynamic weather, random events, and wandering merchant caravans with wild limited-time deals


Draggable minimap, full mobile touch support, and a cheat menu for when you just wanna be a god king

Real-time stat tracking with fancy charts for population, happiness, resources and military power

Automatic battle reports when armies clash, showing casualties and winners

3 difficulty levels so you can either chill build or get absolutely wrecked by enemy kingdoms

It's still messy, there are definitely bugs, and I swear the AI kingdoms are sometimes smarter than I am. I'll be pushing random updates at least every two weeks with new dumb stuff to play with — next up might be airplanes, might be a plague system, might be silly peasant hats.
